## Conventions observed (the house voice as written)

- **Casing.** Sentence case wins by about 4:1 on short labels, buttons and headings (~2,100 sentence-case vs ~490 Title Case, and most of the Title Case is game proper nouns: Integrated Strategies, Control Center, Originite Prime, Special Headhunting). Tool and page names are Title Case product names (DPS Calculator, Squad Builder, Operator Planner). Header navigation is a consistent Title Case island (Tier Lists, Game Data, Story & Lore, My Profile). Older surfaces drift to Title Case: operator detail tabs, stage properties, profile Stats cards, the AuthDialog, the user menu and three of the Appearance settings titles.
- **Retry wording.** "Try again" is the button (about 25 sites). "Retry" shows up 8 times. Body copy says "Try again in a moment" or "in a minute"; only the music gallery says "in a bit".
- **Error pattern.** Title or inline: "Couldn't <verb> <the thing>." (84 sites). Inline panels on operator pages use "The <thing> did not load." The body says what still works or what to do next ("...so tiles show item ids", "Select the link and copy it yourself"). The shared `errorMessage.*` set is strong: it names the cause and a next step.
- **Empty states.** "No <things> match <your filters / that search>." plus one concrete next step ("Try clearing...", "Pick one to..."). Titles have no period; descriptions do.
- **Ellipsis.** Unicode "…" (62 sites) is the norm. Three ASCII dots appear 25 times.
- **Dashes.** No em or en dashes anywhere. A spaced hyphen " - " does the em dash's job in 64 prose strings. That is the same tell, so it is flagged below.
- **No** exclamation marks, "Oops", "Click here", "seamless/powerful/effortless/elevate", "Learn more" or "Get started". "Please" appears 3 times and "successfully" once.
- **Terminology.** "Sign in" / "Sign out" (40+ sites). Mostly US spelling (favorite 24 vs favourite 5). Game terms are used as plain vocabulary (E2, LMD, Orundum, IS, RA, M3).
- **Toasts.** Title with no period ("Couldn't save the squad"), body as a full sentence.
