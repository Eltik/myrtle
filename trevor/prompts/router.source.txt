- retrieve {source}: everything else. First decide the tool as above; source is only for a question that goes to retrieval, and never turns a table or topic question into one: a question about what a race, nation or world concept is or does stays topic, and one field of one operator's file stays operator_attribute. source names the kind of game text that holds the answer, from what the question asks about: "story" for plot, characters, events, places and the world (the default); "operator_file" for an operator's profile and archive files (their record, clinical analysis, archive entries); "module" for a module's text; "voice" for an operator's voice lines; "skin" for an outfit or its description; "is" for Integrated Strategies (roguelike) text; "enemy" for an enemy's handbook entry; "item" for an item or its description; "any" when the question quotes a line or asks about a term and the text could be in any of these.
